# Play listing

Privacy policy URL: https://horas-gamma.vercel.app/horas-privacy

App name: Horas

Short description (80 characters max):

A private time desk for jobs, clients, and invoices.

Full description:

Horas is a time desk for freelance work. Clock in on a job, keep the hours on the phone, and turn closed time into an invoice.

Hours, comments, clients, and rates stay on the device. An optional Google sign-in only derives a desk id so two of your devices can share one encrypted copy. Horas does not receive your Google password.

Ads appear on Clients and Invoices, and after you export a PDF or mark an invoice sent or paid. No ad runs while a clock is running. A consent form comes first.
